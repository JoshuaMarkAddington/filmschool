Two-Week Intensive Course Auditions

A fast, focused two-week intensive for young performers. Learn from industry professionals, master choreography and vocal arrangements built for the stage, and take it all to The Amphitheatre, Campbell Park — right in the heart of Milton Keynes. Come prepared so you can give your very best on the day.

Wear all black with no logos or patterns. Small logos are fine — just avoid anything bold or distracting.

🩰

Footwear for dancing

Wear footwear that is appropriate and safe for dancing.
