Industry-Standard Tools

Adders Film School is currently in development ahead of our 1st September opening. All equipment, software, and studio facilities described on this site reflect our confirmed curriculum and procurement plan, and will be in place for the start of classes. No claims on this site imply that training, equipment, or facilities are currently in use prior to opening.

Cinematography

Students will train on the Blackmagic URSA Cine 12K LF, paired with DZOFilm Arles Prime lenses, giving them hands-on experience with the same resolution and optical quality used on major professional productions. Camera movement will be taught using the Tilta Nucleus-M II wireless follow-focus system, and all setups will be supported on Manfrotto tripods including the 504X Twin Fast fluid head — professional-grade support gear used throughout the industry.

Lighting

Our lighting curriculum is built around Nanlite Forza fixtures (60CR and 300B/500B series), teaching students to shape mood and atmosphere with the same tools used by working gaffers and DPs.

Sound

Production sound modules will use Schoeps MK41, SuperCMIT 2U, and CMC 1U microphones — reference-standard mics used in high-end film and broadcast sound — with Beyerdynamic DT 770 Pro headphones for monitoring, and SYNCO Xtalk wireless headsets for crew communication on set.

Post-Production

Editing, colour grading, and VFX will be taught on DaVinci Resolve Studio, running on workstations built around AMD Threadripper processors and the latest NVIDIA GPUs — the horsepower needed to handle 12K footage without compromise. VFX training will include Ultimate Blockbuster Drive assets from Big Films.

Scriptwriting

Our scriptwriting module is built around WriterDuet, the industry-standard collaborative screenwriting platform used across film and TV development.

Adders Film School opens 1st September 2026. Equipment and facilities listed reflect our confirmed procurement plan for opening day.
